LOCAL DATA AND BACKUPS
The current release stores finance data locally. Device storage can fail, become corrupted, be lost, reset, or be removed during uninstall. Keep appropriate external encrypted backups. Uninstalling/resetting Finora without a separately saved backup may permanently remove local finance records and receipts.

Finora cannot recover a forgotten encrypted-backup password.

IMPORT AND EXPORT

When an export or backup is shared/saved through operating-system UI, protection at the selected destination is controlled by the operating system and recipient application/location.
